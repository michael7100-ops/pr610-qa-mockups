Consistency check — does B-minimal serve the Farmhand goal? Yes, and it's more aligned than the shipped B. Three of the shipped sheet's friction points only made sense as half-built Farmhand scaffolding: the recipient header (pointless ceremony for canned flirts, but the justification for an AI opener), the truncated list (harmful in 1a, but the right instinct that the classic list should be the fallback in 1b), and the refresh icon (wrong for "more options," right for "regenerate"). B-minimal resolves each by tier: in 1a the recipient row stays light, the list is full, and ↻ is retired; in 1b the recipient row becomes meaningful, the list collapses to a labeled opt-out, and ↻ becomes Try-again. One shell, two configurations, no rebuild between phases.
